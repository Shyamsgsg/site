Our Business

Copra, sourced with care

Our core business is sourcing and selling copra, the dried kernel of the mature coconut and the raw material for coconut oil, copra meal and a wide range of food, feed and industrial products.

We work along the supply chain, from growers and aggregators to the mills that crush it, with a focus on consistent quality and dependable delivery.

Sourcing

We buy from growers and established aggregators, building long-standing relationships at origin.

Quality

Copra is valued on moisture, oil content and cleanliness. We focus on properly dried, well-graded copra and check every consignment before it ships.

Logistics

From warehouse to vessel, we coordinate aggregation, storage, documentation and shipping so that every cargo arrives as contracted.

Markets

We supply oil mills and crushers that turn copra into coconut oil and copra meal for the food, oleochemical, personal-care and animal-feed industries.

Steady relationships, careful quality and patient execution: the same principles guide our investments.

Investments

Alongside our copra business, we also invest for the long term, in a concentrated portfolio of companies we understand and are prepared to hold for many years.

Long-term

Investment horizons measured in years, not quarters — compounding requires time and discipline.

Concentrated

A focused book of high-conviction positions rather than a diluted index of ideas.

Research-led

Decisions grounded in business fundamentals, industry structure and how each business allocates its resources.
