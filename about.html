Shyam Sasindran, Director

Shyam Sasindran is the Director of Surabhi Industries Pte Ltd, which he has led since February 2022. Based in Port Moresby, he oversees the company's copra sourcing and trading business and its long-term investments in listed companies.

Shyam holds a Master of Accounting and a Bachelor of Business (Management) from Bond University on the Gold Coast. Before that, he gained public-sector experience with Papua New Guinea's Ministry of Finance and Department of Treasury, working on management and investment strategy. He is also a director of SitaRam Investments Pte Ltd in Singapore.

Outside the business, he follows commodity markets and financial journals closely, and holds an Australian private pilot licence.
